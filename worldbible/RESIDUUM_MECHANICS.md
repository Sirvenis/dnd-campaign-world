# Residuum and Ashening Mechanics

> Homebrew rules for the Aethermere campaign. These rules layer on top of standard D&D 5e/2024 rules and are optional — the DM can use all, some, or none of them.

---

## Residuum

### What It Is

Residuum is the physical remains of the dead gods — their divine blood, soaked into the earth during the Godswar. It appears as a luminescent liquid that seeps from rock and pools in underground caverns. Raw residuum is unpredictable and dangerous. Refined residuum is stable and usable.

### Forms of Residuum

| Form | Description | Value | Ashening Risk |
|------|-------------|-------|---------------|
| **Raw** | Unprocessed liquid from deposits. Glows brightly. Unstable. | 10 gp per flask | High — direct skin contact or inhalation accelerates Ashening |
| **Refined** | Processed into stable form by refineries. Glows softly. | 50 gp per flask | Low — safe to handle, dangerous only with prolonged daily exposure |
| **Crystallised** | Naturally occurring crystals in high-concentration areas. | 100 gp per crystal | Moderate — touching bare crystal causes a minor Ashening tick |
| **Pure** | Found only in the deepest deposits. Liquid light. | 500+ gp per flask | Extreme — minutes of exposure can begin the transformation |
| **Ambient** | Trace amounts in air, soil, water across the world. | N/A | Negligible — the background level everyone lives with |

### Uses of Refined Residuum

- **Spellcasting Focus**: A refined residuum crystal can serve as a spellcasting focus for any class. Casters who use one feel their magic is "stronger" — mechanically, spells cast with a residuum focus have advantage on attack rolls once per long rest (the crystal "remembers" divine power).
- **Healing**: A flask of refined residuum can be consumed to restore 2d4+2 hit points. This is a common medical treatment in Hallow's Reach. Costs 50 gp (or more in shortages).
- **Magic Item Fuel**: Some old-world magic items require residuum to function. A residuum flask powers such an item for 24 hours.
- **Residuum Lamps**: Common lighting in Hallow's Reach. A flask powers a lamp for a week. The glow is faintly blue-white.
- **Crafting**: Magic item creation in Aethermere requires refined residuum as a material component (in addition to standard requirements). The amount varies by item rarity.
- **Resurrection**: True Resurrection and Raise Dead do not work normally in Aethermere (no gods to grant the divine power). However, consuming a large amount of pure residuum (1,000+ gp worth) at the moment of casting can substitute for divine power, making resurrection possible but extremely expensive and risky. The resurrected person always has a faint grey pallor afterwards — a permanent mark.

### Residuum as Currency

In the Ember Coast, refined residuum is used alongside gold. Prices are typically listed in gp (gold pieces) but residuum flasks are accepted as equivalent to 50 gp. In areas far from residuum deposits, only gold is accepted. In the Shatterlands, raw residuum is the preferred currency — gold is heavy and useless in the wilderness.

## The Ashening

### Overview

The Ashening is the progressive transformation caused by excessive residuum exposure. It is the central horror of Aethermere — the personal cost of the world's power source.

### Ashening Stages

The Ashening progresses through five stages. Each stage is tracked as an **Ashening Score** that the DM records secretly (the player may know they're at risk but shouldn't know the exact number until symptoms appear).

| Stage | Score | Symptoms | Mechanical Effects |
|-------|-------|----------|-------------------|
| **0 — Clean** | 0 | None | No effects |
| **1 — Touched** | 1-10 | Faint grey pallor after residuum contact. Fades after a few hours away from the source. | None. Detectable by Henna Moss or similar druids. |
| **2 — Marked** | 11-25 | Permanent faint grey tint to skin. Eyes occasionally flash with faint light in darkness. Sense of "resonance" near residuum deposits. | Advantage on detecting residuum (sensing deposits, identifying residuum-based magic). Disadvantage on saving throws against residuum-based effects (you're more attuned, so more vulnerable). |
| **3 — Changed** | 26-50 | Skin is clearly grey. Eyes glow faintly and constantly. Hair may turn white or become slightly translucent. Patches of skin may develop a crystalline texture. | +1 to spell attack rolls and spell save DC (residuum power is flowing through you). Your touch can be detected by spiritual senses (ancestor spirits, the Resonant). Animals are uneasy around you. |
| **4 — Fading** | 51-80 | Significant physical transformation. Crystalline patches spread. Limbs may become partially translucent. Constant low-level magical aura. Personality begins to shift — emotions flatten, memories become less personal, a growing sense of detachment from mortal concerns. | +2 to spell attack rolls and spell save DC. Resistance to radiant and necrotic damage. Vulnerability to psychic damage (the mind is being eroded). Disadvantage on Wisdom and Charisma saving throws (identity is slipping). NPC reactions shift from sympathy to fear. |
| **5 — Hollow** | 81+ | Complete loss of self. The body becomes a vessel of raw divine energy — powerful, mindless, and dangerous. The person is gone. | Becomes an NPC under DM control. Use the Hollow stat block (below). There is no known way to reverse this stage. |

### Gaining Ashening Points

Ashening points accumulate through exposure. The DM tracks these secretly.

| Exposure | Points Gained | Notes |
|----------|--------------|-------|
| Handling refined residuum (daily work) | 1 per week | Refinery workers, lamp-lighters |
| Direct skin contact with raw residuum | 1 per hour | Miners without protective equipment |
| Inhalation of residuum dust (Ash Fields) | 1 per 10 minutes | Without a residuum mask |
| Touching crystallised residuum | 1 per touch | Brief contact |
| Exposure to pure residuum | 1 per minute | The deep Godvein, the Drowned Temple lake |
| Casting spells using residuum focus | 0 (refined) / 1 per 10 casts (raw) | Regular spellcasting with refined residuum is safe |
| Consuming refined residuum for healing | 0 | Medical doses are safe |
| Consuming raw or pure residuum | 1d4 per dose | Extremely dangerous, but grants temporary hit points and a surge of magical power |
| Being hit by a Hollow's Ashen Touch | 1d4 | Combat exposure |

### Reducing Ashening Points

The Ashening can be slowed and partially reversed, but not easily.

| Method | Reduction | Notes |
|--------|-----------|-------|
| Time away from residuum | 1 per month of zero exposure | Requires leaving the Ember Coast or living in the Saltmarch |
| Greyveil Monastery meditation | 1 per week of intensive training | Requires travelling to the monastery and training with Abbot Theren |
| Henna Moss's herbal treatments | 1 per treatment (weekly max) | Only works at Stage 1-2. Costs 10 gp per treatment. |
| Greater Restoration spell | 1d4 | Cast at the spell's normal cost. Only works at Stage 1-3. |
| No known method reverses Stage 4+ | — | At Stage 4 (Fading), the transformation is self-sustaining. The body generates its own residuum. Only divine intervention (which is impossible — the gods are dead) could reverse it. |

### The Greyveil Technique (Monk Meditation)

Abbot Theren teaches a meditation technique that slows the Ashening. Characters who spend a week training at Greyveil Monastery gain the following benefit:

**Greyveil Meditation (Prerequisite: Training at Greyveil Monastery)**
- When you would gain Ashening points, you may make a Wisdom saving throw (DC = current Ashening score). On a success, you gain half the points (rounded down).
- You may use this ability once per long rest.
- This benefit is permanent once learned.

## The Hollow Stat Block

Use this for NPCs who have reached Stage 5 of the Ashening.

```
HOLLOW
Large aberration, typically neutral (was the creature's original alignment)
Armor Class: 16 (natural armour — crystalline skin)
Hit Points: 95 (10d8 + 50)
Speed: 30 ft., fly 30 ft. (hover)

STR  DEX  CON  INT  WIS  CHA
18 (+4)  14 (+2)  20 (+5)  3 (-4)  6 (-2)  8 (-1)

Saving Throws: Con +9, Wis +2
Damage Immunities: radiant, necrotic
Damage Resistances: bludgeoning, piercing, and slashing from nonmagical attacks
Damage Vulnerabilities: psychic
Condition Immunities: charmed, frightened, paralyzed, stunned
Senses: blindsight 60 ft. (residuum sense), passive Perception 8
Languages: None (may whisper fragments of past speech)
Challenge: 5 (1,800 XP)

Ashen Touch. Melee spell attack: +7 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target.
Hit: 22 (4d8 + 4) radiant damage, and the target gains 1d4 Ashening points.

Residuum Burst (Recharge 5-6). The Hollow releases a burst of raw divine
energy. Each creature within 20 feet must make a DC 17 Constitution saving
throw, taking 28 (8d6) radiant damage on a failed save, or half as much on
a successful one. Creatures who fail also gain 1 Ashening point.

Crystalline Body. When the Hollow is subjected to bludgeoning, piercing, or
slashing damage from a nonmagical weapon, the weapon takes 1d6 radiant
damage. If this reduces the weapon to 0 hit points, it shatters.

Hover. The Hollow can hover and move through the air. It doesn't provoke
opportunity attacks when it flies out of an enemy's reach.

Unstable Form. When the Hollow drops to 0 hit points, it detonates in a
final burst of energy. Each creature within 10 feet must make a DC 15
Dexterity saving throw, taking 14 (4d6) radiant damage on a failed save.
```

### Hollow Variants

- **Newly Hollowed** (CR 3): Reduced hit points (52), no Residuum Burst, Ashen Touch deals 2d8 radiant. Still resembles their former self — may wear old clothes, may wander to familiar places. Tragic rather than terrifying.
- **Ancient Hollow** (CR 8): Has been Hollow for decades. Fully crystalline. Larger (Huge). Hit Points 142. Ashen Touch deals 5d8 radiant. Gains a third ability:
  - **Resonant Wail (Recharge 6)**: The Hollow emits a psychic scream. Each creature within 30 feet must make a DC 16 Wisdom saving throw or be stunned for 1 minute. A stunned creature can repeat the save at the end of each of its turns.

## Residuum Items

### Residuum Mask
- **Cost**: 25 gp
- **Wearable**: Face covering, covers nose and mouth
- **Effect**: Filters residuum dust from the air. While wearing a mask, inhalation-based Ashening accumulation is reduced to 1 per hour instead of 1 per 10 minutes. Essential equipment for exploring the Ash Fields or working in dusty mines.
- **Limitation**: Needs new filters every 8 hours of use (5 gp per filter). A mask with a spent filter provides no protection.

### Residuum Lamp
- **Cost**: 15 gp (lamp) + 50 gp per flask (1 week of fuel)
- **Effect**: Provides bright light in a 30-foot radius and dim light for an additional 30 feet. The light is blue-white and slightly eerie. Does not attract insects (the residuum repels them).
- **Limitation**: If a lamp is broken, everyone within 5 feet gains 1 Ashening point from the released residuum vapour.

### Residuum Detector (Cartographers' Guild issue)
- **Cost**: 200 gp (rare, Guild-controlled)
- **Effect**: A brass device with a residuum crystal suspended in a liquid medium. When brought within 30 feet of a significant residuum deposit, the crystal glows brighter and the liquid shifts colour (white-gold for raw, blue for refined, green for impure/dangerous). Can also detect Ashening in creatures — hold it near a person and the crystal will glow if they're Stage 2 or higher.
- **Limitation**: Fragile. Drops or impacts have a 25% chance to break it.

### Residuum Armour (Rare)
- **Cost**: 500 gp + 1 flask refined residuum per week to maintain
- **Effect**: Leather armour treated with refined residuum. AC 12 + Dex modifier (max 2). The wearer has resistance to radiant damage. The armour glows faintly, providing dim light in a 5-foot radius.
- **Limitation**: The wearer gains 1 Ashening point per week of continuous use. The armour must be "fed" one flask of refined residuum per week or it becomes normal leather armour.

### Purified Residuum Vial (Very Rare)
- **Cost**: 1,000+ gp
- **Effect**: A vial of residuum that has been purified through a process known only to the Silver Isles refiners. When consumed, it restores all hit points and cures any disease, curse, or condition (except the Ashening). It also grants the consumer temporary maximum hit points equal to their level for 1 hour.
- **Limitation**: Consuming it causes 1d4 Ashening points (even purified residuum is still divine blood). Each additional vial consumed within a month increases the Ashening gain by 1d4 (cumulative).

## DM Guidelines for Running the Ashening

### Keep It Secret, Keep It Tense

Track Ashening scores privately. Don't tell players their exact number — let them discover it through symptoms, NPC reactions, and Henna Moss's screenings. The uncertainty is part of the horror.

### Don't Punish Casual Play

The Ashening should not make players afraid to use magic or explore. The accumulation rates are designed so that normal adventuring (casting spells with a residuum focus, using residuum lamps, healing with residuum flasks) produces negligible Ashening. The risk comes from specific high-exposure situations: deep mine exploration, Ash Fields, fighting Hollows, handling raw residuum.

### Make It a Choice, Not a Punishment

The best Ashening moments are when the player *chooses* the risk. "I know touching the pure residuum will advance my Ashening, but I need the power to save my friend." That's drama. Forced Ashening from random encounters is less interesting.

### Use NPCs to Show the Stages

- Stage 1-2: Refinery workers, some miners. Common. Sympathetic.
- Stage 3: Brother Ashen Kael. Respected by some, feared by others.
- Stage 4: The Ashen Choir members. Frightening but still recognisably people.
- Stage 5: Hollows. Tragic monsters. Show the humanity that was lost — a Hollow that still wears a wedding ring, one that returns to its old home, one that whispers a name it can't remember.

### The Real Horror Is Agency

The Ashening is scary not because it's random but because it's *chosen*. Every time a character touches residuum for power, they're choosing to give up a piece of themselves. That's the theme of the world: what are you willing to lose to save what matters?
