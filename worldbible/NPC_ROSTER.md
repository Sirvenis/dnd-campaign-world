# NPC Roster — Ember Coast

> Full personality descriptions, motivations, and stat block references for key NPCs. DM-only document.

## Faction Leaders

### Lady Seren Hallow — The Hallow Compact

**Race/Class:** Human (Noble) / Aristocrat (use Noble stat block, MM)
**Age:** 63
**Appearance:** Silver-haired, straight-backed, always dressed in dark colours with residuum-thread embroidery that gives her clothing a faint glow. Her eyes are sharp and brown. She wears a signet ring with the Hallow crest — a mountain with a vein of light running through it.

**Personality:** Pragmatic, controlled, and deeply tired. She's held the Compact together for thirty years through sheer force of will and political cunning. She genuinely cares about Hallow's Reach and its people — but she cares about stability more. She's seen what happens when order breaks down (she was a young woman during a famine caused by a trade disruption). She would rather manage a slow decline than risk a violent upheaval.

**Secret:** She knows the Godvein is running low. She's had the estimates for two years. She's been quietly trying to find solutions — sending agents to the Shatterlands to find new deposits, corresponding with Silver Isles engineers about alternative refining methods — but nothing has worked. She's terrified that if the truth gets out, the city will descend into chaos before anyone can fix it.

**Role in Campaign:** The powerful ally who's keeping a secret. She can provide the party with resources, authority, and information — but she's also the obstacle who won't admit the crisis is real. The party may end up working for her, opposing her, or trying to force her to go public.

**Voice:** Measured, precise, slightly formal. Never raises her voice. Uses "we" when she means "I." Pauses before answering difficult questions — the pause is deliberate, not uncertainty.

**Key Quote:** "The world has been ending for three hundred years. Our job is to make sure it ends slowly enough that people can live their lives in the meantime."

### Brother Ashen Kael — The Ashborn

**Race/Class:** Human (Partial Ashened) / Cleric (Life Domain) 8th level
**Age:** 41
**Appearance:** Tall and thin. His left arm, from the shoulder down, is translucent crystalline material — you can see light pulsing through it like blood through veins. His eyes are a faint blue-white glow. The rest of him looks human, though his skin has a greyish pallor. He wears simple grey robes and walks barefoot.

**Personality:** Compassionate, charismatic, and genuinely believes he's right. He was a priest of the Pale Theocracy who lost his faith when his prayers went unanswered for years. He came to the Ember Coast looking for meaning and found it — not in the gods, but in the residuum. He experienced the Ashening not as corruption but as awakening. He wants to share that with others. He's not a cult leader — he doesn't coerce or manipulate. He simply offers people something to believe in, and many are desperate enough to accept.

**The Problem:** He may be wrong. The Ashening he experienced was partial and slow. He's retained his mind and his empathy. But others who've followed his path have not been so lucky. He's seen people Hollow — fully lose themselves — after attempting the "awakening" he taught them. He tells himself these were cases of impure residuum or improper technique. He may be lying to himself.

**Secret:** He's accelerating. His crystalline arm is growing — the Ashening is progressing despite his claims that it can be controlled. He's hiding this from his followers. He doesn't know what happens when it reaches his heart.

**Role in Campaign:** The charismatic reformer who may be leading people to salvation or destruction. The party may be drawn to his vision, repelled by his methods, or tasked with stopping him. He's not a villain — he's a tragic figure with a genuinely good heart and a potentially catastrophic philosophy.

**Voice:** Warm, gentle, slightly hoarse. Speaks in metaphors about light and growth. Frequently touches his crystalline arm when he's thinking. Makes eye contact that's intense but not aggressive.

**Key Quote:** "The gods are dead. But their power lives — in the earth, in the water, in us. We can let it fade, or we can become what they were. I know what I choose."

### Commander Varek Pale — The Order of the Pale Flame

**Race/Class:** Human / Fighter (Battle Master) 7th level
**Age:** 45
**Appearance:** Solidly built, weathered face, military bearing. He has a burn scar across his left cheek — not from fire, but from concentrated residuum exposure (the scar has a faint permanent glow). He wears practical armour, no ornamentation. His weapon is a plain longsword with a notch in the blade from the strike that killed his first Hollow.

**Personality:** Direct, intense, and driven by grief. He's not a fanatic — he's a man who lost everything and found a cause. His wife and daughter became Hollows after working in a residuum refinery. He had to kill his wife when she turned violent. His daughter is still alive — a Hollow, contained in a sealed chamber in Pale Flame Keep. He visits her every week. She doesn't recognise him anymore.

He has a legitimate point: residuum is dangerous, the Ashening is real, and the economy is built on a substance that destroys people. But his solution — destroy all of it — would cause immediate suffering for millions. He knows this. He's decided the short-term suffering is worth it to prevent the long-term catastrophe. Whether he's right is a question the campaign should ask.

**Secret:** His daughter is still alive as a Hollow. He's keeping her contained, not killed, because he hopes the Ashening can be reversed. He's secretly funding research into a cure — which puts him in indirect contact with the Cartographers' Guild and even the Resonant, though he'd never admit it.

**Role in Campaign:** The antagonist who might be right. He'll raid refineries, sabotage mines, and attack residuum shipments — putting him in direct conflict with the party if they're working with the Compact. But his cause is understandable, and his grief is real. The party might end up sympathising with him, trying to find a middle ground, or having to stop him by force.

**Voice:** Flat, clipped, military. Doesn't waste words. Doesn't threaten — he states what he's going to do and does it. When he talks about his family, his voice drops almost to a whisper.

**Key Quote:** "I held my wife as she stopped being my wife. I watched her eyes go bright and empty. I put my sword through her chest while she smiled at me with a stranger's face. Don't tell me about the benefits of residuum."

### Master Cartographer Inera Thale — The Cartographers' Guild

**Race/Class:** Human / Ranger (Gloom Stalker) 9th level
**Age:** 43
**Appearance:** Lean, weathered, and missing her left eye (worn in a leather patch) and the last three fingers of her right hand. Her remaining hair is cropped short and grey. She wears practical explorer's clothing, always carries a case of maps and surveying tools, and has a short sword that's seen more use as a machete than a weapon.

**Personality:** Curious, calm, and quietly haunted. She's spent twenty years exploring the Shatterlands and has seen things that would break most people. She's not fearless — she's just learned to function alongside her fear. She's the most knowledgeable person in the Ember Coast about the old world, the Shatterlands, and the Godswar's physical effects. She's also the most neutral figure in the region — every faction trusts her because she has no agenda beyond understanding.

**Secret:** She found something in the Drowned Temple's deep chamber three years ago. She won't talk about it. What she found: a mural depicting the Godswar's true cause. The gods didn't just fight each other — something came from outside reality (the Far Realm) and influenced them, drove them to war, or was the actual target of the war. The mural is fragmentary and ambiguous, but it suggests the Godswar wasn't a civil war. It was a defence that failed. She's been sitting on this because she doesn't fully understand it and she's afraid of what happens if she's wrong — or if she's right.

**Role in Campaign:** The quest-giver, mentor, and secret-keeper. She'll hire the party for Shatterlands expeditions, provide maps and lore, and slowly — as she learns to trust them — share pieces of what she found. She's the path to the campaign's deepest mystery.

**Voice:** Quiet, measured, with a dry wit. Speaks in precise descriptions. Gets uncomfortable when the conversation turns to the Drowned Temple — changes the subject smoothly. Stares at maps when she's thinking.

**Key Quote:** "I've mapped every crack, every ruin, every gravity anomaly in the Shatterlands. I know that land better than I know my own face. And there is one place I will not go again. Not yet. Not without good reason and good company."

### Sela Resonant — The Resonant

**Race/Class:** Human / Sorcerer (Divine Soul) 3rd level
**Age:** 20
**Appearance:** Small, slight, with dark hair and dark skin. She looks ordinary — a miner's daughter, nothing special. Except for her eyes: when she's "listening" (sensing residuum), her irises shift from brown to a deep, luminous gold. It's subtle in daylight, striking in darkness. She wears working clothes and has calloused hands from years in the Godvein.

**Personality:** Shy, earnest, and overwhelmed. She didn't ask for this. She was a miner who heard something, and now people are looking to her for answers she doesn't have. She's smart but uneducated. She's brave but frightened. She genuinely believes the voices are real — not hallucination, not Far Realm influence, but genuine fragments of dead gods — but she can't prove it, and neither can anyone else.

**The Voices:** What she hears is fragmentary — not words, but emotions, images, a sense of... presence. It's strongest near concentrated residuum. She can sense deposits, detect early Ashening in people, and sometimes — rarely — catch glimpses of memories that aren't hers. The memories are always of the Godswar. They're always terrifying. And they always cut off at the same moment: just before the end.

**Secret:** She's heard one clear word. Just once. A name. She doesn't know what it means, but it felt like a god's name — the first one spoken in 300 years. She hasn't told anyone. She's afraid of what it means.

**Role in Campaign:** The wildcard. She's the most important person in the Ember Coast and doesn't know it. Every faction wants to use her — the Ashborn see her as proof of their philosophy, the Pale Flame see her as a dangerous heretic, the Compact see her as a political tool, the Cartographers see her as a research subject, and the Resonant see her as their leader. The party's relationship with her will shape the campaign.

**Voice:** Quiet, halting, grows more confident when talking about what she hears. Apologises a lot. Speaks in simple, direct language — no metaphors, no fancy words. When she describes the voices, she becomes intensely focused, almost trance-like.

**Key Quote:** "I can't explain it. It's like... you know when someone is standing behind you, and you can feel them there before you turn around? It's like that. But it's everywhere. And it's sad. Whatever it is, it's so sad."

## Supporting NPCs

### Armin Gainsbury — Emberwood Village

**Race/Class:** Human (Commoner) / Retired Adventurer (former Fighter 4)
**Age:** 55
**Appearance:** Stocky, bearded, missing his left hand (lost to a Hollow). Runs the Black Root Tavern and the supply store next door.

**Personality:** Gruff, generous, and nosy. He knows every adventurer who passes through Emberwood and keeps track of who's reliable and who's trouble. He's a natural information broker — not for money, but because he genuinely cares about keeping people alive. He'll warn parties about dangers, share rumours, and point them toward work.

**Role:** Quest-giver, information source, and the party's first contact in Emberwood. He's the "starting NPC" who eases them into the world.

**Key Quote:** "Last group that went to the Broken Spire didn't come back. The group before that did, but they were missing a man and had a story I didn't want to hear twice. You still want to go? Fine. Buy some rope first."

### Henna Moss — Emberwood Village

**Race/Class:** Halfling / Druid (Circle of the Land) 5th level
**Age:** 38
**Appearance:** Small, round-faced, with dirt under her fingernails and leaves in her hair. She maintains the Shrine of Small Things — not a temple to any god, but a quiet space dedicated to "the small good things that survive."

**Personality:** Practical, warm, and blunt. She's the closest thing Emberwood has to a doctor. She can sense early-stage Ashening by touch — a tingling sensation when she handles someone whose body is starting to accumulate residuum. She offers this service free to adventurers (they need it).

**Role:** Healing, Ashening detection, and the party's first exposure to the druidic perspective on residuum. She sees it as part of nature now — neither good nor evil, just something the world has to live with.

**Key Quote:** "The earth absorbed the gods' blood. The plants grew in it. The animals drank it. We're all part residuum now, whether we like it or not. The question isn't how to get rid of it. The question is how to live with it without losing ourselves."

### Abbot Theren — Greyveil Monastery

**Race/Class:** Human / Monk (Way of the Open Hand) 8th level
**Age:** 60
**Appearance:** Shaved head, thin, with faint blue luminescence in his eyes (early Ashening, slowed by decades of meditation). He wears simple grey robes and walks barefoot even in snow.

**Personality:** Serene, patient, and quietly funny. He's been slowing his own Ashening for thirty years through meditation techniques he developed. He teaches these techniques to anyone who asks, regardless of faction. He believes the Ashening can be managed — not cured, but controlled — through discipline and self-awareness.

**Role:** Resource for characters dealing with Ashening. He can teach techniques that slow the progression (mechanically: a way to reduce Ashening accumulation). He also knows old stories about the gods that predate the Godswar — oral traditions passed down through the monastery for centuries.

**Key Quote:** "The blood of gods flows in your veins. It flows in mine. The question is not whether it changes you — everything changes you. The question is whether you guide the change or let it guide you."

### Mara Reedwatch — Saltmarch

**Race/Class:** Human / Druid (Circle of the Moon) 6th level
**Age:** 90
**Appearance:** Wiry, weathered, and sharp-eyed. She looks like a bundle of sticks that somehow learned to walk and developed opinions. She wears reed-woven clothing and carries a staff made from driftwood and bone.

**Personality:** Sharp-tongued, suspicious of outsiders, and fiercely protective of her village. She maintains the ancestor binding ritual that keeps the village's dead as protective spirits. She's been doing this for sixty years. She knows more about the broken afterlife than most scholars — she's been working with it directly her whole life.

**Role:** Contact for the Saltmarch adventures. She can explain the ancestor binding tradition, provide information about restless spirits, and serve as a druidic counterpoint to the residuum-focused factions. She's also the one who asks the party to deal with the livestock killer in Mire's End.

**Key Quote:** "The dead don't go anywhere. That's the problem. So we give them somewhere to be. We give them purpose. A spirit with purpose is a guardian. A spirit without purpose is a haunting. Simple as that."
