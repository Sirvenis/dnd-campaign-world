# Session 1: The Road to Emberwood

> The first session of the Aethermere campaign. Designed for 3-4 characters at level 1.
> Uses the "five-room dungeon" structure adapted for a travel/escort opening.

---

## Session Overview

**Setup**: The player characters are newcomers to the Ember Coast, hired to guard a supply caravan from the distant city of Altberg to Emberwood Village. The journey is the tutorial — it introduces the world, the key themes, and the basic mechanics, all through action rather than exposition.

**Duration**: One session (3-4 hours)

**Objectives**:
- Introduce the world through experience, not lecture
- Give players their first combat encounter
- Let players make their first meaningful choice
- End with a hook that leads into Session 2

---

## The Opening

### Scene 1: The Caravan Assembly (15 minutes)

**Location**: Altberg, a generic city off the map. The party has been hired by **Eren Marlowe**, a provisioner (use the stat block for a Guard, but friendly and chatty). Eren is loading a wagon with supplies for Emberwood Village — food, tools, residuum lamp fuel, medical supplies.

**What Happens**:
- The party meets Eren and each other. Eren is chatty and asks each character why they're going to the Ember Coast. This is the in-character introduction scene — let players describe their characters and their reasons.
- Eren shares useful information in a natural way:
  - "Emberwood's the last stop before the Shatterlands. Everything beyond it is broken ground — gravity goes sideways, the air glows, and things live out there that used to be people."
  - "The residuum trade keeps the whole coast running. Hallow's Reach refines it, the Silver Isles buy it, and everyone in between depends on it. But you don't want to touch the raw stuff. There's a thing called the Ashening..."
  - "It's a three-week journey. The roads are mostly safe. Mostly."
- Eren pays each character 25 gold in advance, with 25 more on arrival.

**DM Notes**: This is the exposition scene disguised as conversation. Let Eren do the talking — don't monologue. If players ask questions, Eren answers with what a regular person would know (not DM secrets). Keep it to 15 minutes max.

### Scene 2: The Road (30-40 minutes)

**Location**: The Old Trade Road, heading east toward the Ember Coast. Three weeks of travel compressed into a brief montage with one encounter.

**The Montage**: Describe the journey in broad strokes — the landscape changing from green hills to flatter, drier terrain. The air taking on a faint quality (ambient residuum). The party getting to know each other around the campfire at night.

Ask each player: "What does your character do during the long evenings on the road?" This is a roleplaying prompt — let them establish personality through small moments.

**The Encounter (Bandits or Wildlife)**: On the second week, the caravan is threatened. Choose based on party composition:

**Option A: Bandits (3-4 Bandits, CR 1 each)**
- A group of road agents blocks the path with a fallen tree
- They want the supplies, not blood — they'll negotiate first
- If combat breaks out, they fight until two are down, then flee
- This is a simple combat tutorial — let players learn the system

**Option B: Residuum-Crazed Wildlife (1 Dire Wolf, CR 1)**
- A wolf that's been drinking from a residuum-contaminated stream
- Its eyes glow faintly blue. It's aggressive and doesn't fear fire
- More dangerous than a normal wolf — its bite deals an extra 1d4 radiant damage
- After the fight, Eren explains: "That's what the Ashening does to animals. Imagine what it does to people."

**DM Notes**: Keep the encounter simple. This is session 1 — the goal is to learn the combat system, not to challenge experienced players. If the party is struggling, Eren can help (he has a crossbow and isn't useless).

### Scene 3: The Ember Fog (10-15 minutes)

**Location**: Approaching the Ember Coast, end of the third week.

**What Happens**: The party enters the "ember fog" — the faintly luminescent fog that covers the coastal region. It's harmless but unnerving. Visibility drops to 60 feet. The air tastes faintly of copper.

- Characters with residuum sensitivity (sorcerers, the Resonant, anyone with Ashening Stage 2+) feel a tingling sensation
- Characters with passive Perception 13+ notice the fog is thicker in some areas — those are ambient residuum concentrations
- Eren explains: "That's the ember fog. Divine blood in the air. Don't worry about it — you'd have to breathe it for years before anything happened. It's the concentrated stuff you need to fear."

**Atmosphere**: This is the tone-setting scene. The world is beautiful and dangerous. The fog is eerie but not threatening. Let players feel the atmosphere.

### Scene 4: Emberwood Village (30-40 minutes)

**Location**: Emberwood Village. The caravan arrives at dusk.

**What Happens**: The party delivers Eren and his supplies. They're paid their remaining 25 gold each. Eren introduces them to **Armin Gainsbury** at the Black Root Tavern.

**Armin Gainsbury** (use NPC Roster):
- Gruff, missing a hand, runs the tavern and supply store
- Buys the party a round of ale and asks what they're looking for
- Shares rumours and hooks (see below)
- Points out the Cartographers' Outpost if they want mapping work
- Mentions Henna Moss at the Shrine if anyone needs healing

**Rumours Armin Shares** (players can pick which to follow):
1. "The Broken Spire — a tower in the Shatterlands, sheared in half during the Godswar. Five floors, partially collapsed. Some say there's a working old-world mechanism inside. Last group that went didn't come back." (Hook for Session 2)
2. "Hallow's Reach is looking for guards. Lady Hallow's been hiring adventurers for 'special assignments.' Pays well, but you work for her, which means you work by her rules." (Hook for faction involvement)
3. "There's something killing livestock down in Mire's End — one of the Saltmarch villages. Old Mara Reedwatch sent word asking for help. Probably just a marsh beast, but the villagers are spooked." (Hook for a simpler, lower-danger adventure)
4. "Brother Kael's been preaching in the village square. Says the Ashening isn't a curse — it's a gift. Says we should embrace it. Half the village thinks he's a prophet. The other half thinks he's dangerous. Make up your own mind." (Hook for faction encounter)
5. "The Cartographers are looking for survey teams. They pay in gold and maps — and their maps are worth more than the gold if you're heading into the Shatterlands." (Hook for Guild work)

### Scene 5: The Choice and the Hook (10-15 minutes)

**What Happens**: The party decides what to do next. This is their first meaningful choice. Whatever they pick leads to Session 2.

**If they choose the Broken Spire**: Armin gives directions and sells them a rough map (10 gp). He warns them to bring rope and residuum masks. Session 2 begins with the journey into the Shatterlands.

**If they choose Hallow's Reach**: Armin arranges passage on a supply wagon heading to the city (1 day travel). Session 2 begins with arrival in Hallow's Reach and a meeting with a Compact representative.

**If they choose Mire's End**: Armin gives directions to the Saltmarch (3 days travel south). Session 2 begins with arrival at the village and meeting Mara Reedwatch.

**If they want to hear Brother Kael**: He's preaching the next morning. Session 2 begins with his speech and the party's first faction encounter.

**If they choose Cartographers' work**: Torch Maren at the outpost hires them to map a section of the Shatterlands near the Broken Spire. Session 2 begins with the expedition.

**The Closing Scene**: Whatever they choose, end the session with a quiet moment. The party is in the Black Root Tavern or around a campfire. The ember fog glows outside. Someone asks: "So. What are we doing tomorrow?"

End session. Let the anticipation build.

## Session 1 Checklist for the DM

### Before the Session
- [ ] Read the Player's Guide and make copies for each player
- [ ] Read the World Bible sections on the Ember Coast and factions
- [ ] Prepare character sheets or have D&D Beyond ready
- [ ] Print or display the Ember Coast map
- [ ] Read the NPC Roster entries for Eren Marlowe and Armin Gainsbury
- [ ] Prepare the bandit or dire wolf encounter (stat blocks ready)
- [ ] Have the rumour list ready (5 hooks from Armin)

### During the Session
- [ ] Open with the caravan assembly — let players introduce themselves
- [ ] Keep Eren's exposition short — 15 minutes max
- [ ] Run the travel montage — ask each player what they do in the evenings
- [ ] Run the combat encounter — keep it simple, let players learn the system
- [ ] Describe the ember fog with atmosphere — this sets the tone
- [ ] Arrive at Emberwood — introduce Armin and the village
- [ ] Present the 5 rumour hooks — let the party choose
- [ ] End with a quiet moment and the question "What are we doing tomorrow?"

### After the Session
- [ ] Note which hook the party chose — prep that for Session 2
- [ ] Record any character details, names, or relationships that emerged
- [ ] Note player preferences (did they enjoy combat? roleplay? exploration?)
- [ ] If anyone showed interest in a faction, note it for future sessions

## Session 1 Stat Blocks (Quick Reference)

### Eren Marlowe (Provisioner)
```
Use Guard stat block (MM), but:
- Alignment: Neutral Good
- Personality: Chatty, opinionated, kind
- Equipment: Crossbow, dagger, merchant's scale, wagon
- Will help in combat but is not a fighter — fires crossbow from cover
```

### Residuum-Crazed Dire Wolf (CR 1)
```
Large beast, unaligned
Armor Class: 14 (natural armour)
Hit Points: 37 (5d10 + 10)
Speed: 50 ft.
STR  DEX  CON  INT  WIS  CHA
17 (+3) 15 (+2) 15 (+2) 3 (-4) 12 (+1) 7 (-2)
Skills: Perception +3, Stealth +4
Senses: passive Perception 13
Languages: None
Challenge: 1 (200 XP)

Keen Hearing and Smell. The wolf has advantage on Wisdom (Perception)
checks that rely on hearing or smell.

Pack Tactics. The wolf has advantage on an attack roll against a
creature if at least one of the wolf's allies is within 5 feet of the
creature and the ally isn't incapacitated.

Bite. Melee Weapon Attack: +5 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target.
Hit: 8 (1d10 + 3) piercing damage plus 2 (1d4) radiant damage
(residuum contamination). If the target is a creature, it must
succeed on a DC 13 Strength saving throw or be knocked prone.

Residuum Glow. The wolf's eyes and saliva glow faintly blue. It
provides dim light in a 5-foot radius. It does not fear fire and
is immune to the frightened condition.
```

### Armin Gainsbury (Tavern Keeper)
```
Use Commoner stat block (MM), but:
- Alignment: Neutral Good
- HP: 18 (he was tougher in his adventuring days)
- Equipment: Club (under the bar), ring of keys
- Knowledge: Knows the Shatterlands, all local rumours, and every
  adventurer who's passed through in the last decade
- Will not fight unless the tavern is attacked directly
```

## Session 2 Prep Notes (Based on Party Choice)

### If Broken Spire:
- Prep the Broken Spire location (5 floors, partially collapsed)
- Include: a working old-world mechanism (communication device), a Hollow nest, a gravity anomaly room, and a residuum crystal cache
- Recommended level: 2 (give them a level-up after Session 1)

### If Hallow's Reach:
- Prep the city in more detail (use the Gazetteer)
- Include: a meeting with Captain Dren Hallow, a tour of the city, a chance to see the Godvein entrance, and a "job offer" from Lady Hallow
- Introduce the faction tensions through NPC encounters

### If Mire's End:
- Prep the Saltmarch village and the livestock killer (a residuum-mutated crocodile, CR 2)
- Include: meeting Mara Reedwatch, learning about ancestor binding, and a marsh combat encounter
- Good for parties who want a simpler, more rural start

### If Brother Kael:
- Prep his speech (about 3 paragraphs of in-character preaching)
- Include: the party's first exposure to Ashening philosophy, a heckler in the crowd (Pale Flame sympathiser), and a chance to talk to Kael afterwards
- Leads into faction dynamics early

### If Cartographers' Guild:
- Prep Torch Maren (2nd-level ranger, practical, quiet)
- Include: a survey contract, a map of the target area, and the journey to the Shatterlands edge
- The survey area overlaps with the Broken Spire — gives them a reason to explore it
