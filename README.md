# D&D Campaign World — Aethermere

An original fantasy world for tabletop D&D 5e/2024 play. The gods are dead, their blood is in the earth, and mortals must decide what comes next.

## Structure

```
worldbible/     — The world bible: cosmology, history, geography, factions, pantheon, campaign arc
campaign/       — Campaign-specific planning: arc outlines, session notes, adventure locations
sessions/       — Individual session prep and notes
maps/           — World, region, and battle maps
```

## World Concept

The gods went to war three centuries ago and killed each other. Their divine blood soaked into the earth, creating **residuum** — the raw power that fuels what remains of magic, industry, and healing. But the residuum is finite, it's dangerous (prolonged exposure causes the Ashening), and every faction has a different idea about what to do with what's left.
